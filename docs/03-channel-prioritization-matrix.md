# Channel prioritisation matrix by market maturity

Not every channel deserves money in every market. The right mix depends on how well the market knows you, how much proof you have, and how predictable demand is. Use the matrix to choose **two primary channels** and **at most two test channels** per market.

## Maturity stages

| Stage | Signals |
|---|---|
| Launch | Low brand awareness, few or no local references, CAC unknown, funnel conversion unmeasured |
| Traction | Some customers and references, CAC measured on one or two channels, cohort data starting |
| Scale | Known brand in the category, predictable CAC, strong references, retention proven |

## Matrix (B2B marketplace example)

Legend: **P** = primary, **T** = test, **S** = support (low spend, always on), **-** = not yet.

| Channel | Launch | Traction | Scale | Why it changes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paid search (high-intent keywords) | P | P | S | Captures existing demand early; saturates as you scale |
| Paid social (prospecting) | T | P | P | Needs proof and creative learnings before it pays back |
| Paid social (retargeting) | S | S | S | Cheap and always useful, but capped by traffic |
| Sales enablement and field sales | P | P | S | References and trust matter most when the brand is unknown |
| Industry and trade events | T | P | S | Fast access to buyers; expensive per lead once demand is predictable |
| CRM and lifecycle | S | P | P | Value grows with the customer base |
| Partnerships and distributors | T | T | P | Slow to set up, then compounding |
| Content and SEO | S | S | P | Long lead time; start early, expect returns at scale |
| Influencers and trade media | - | T | S | Useful once there is a story and a proof point to amplify |
| Referral programme | - | T | P | Needs a satisfied base first |

## Scoring a channel for a specific market

When the default matrix does not fit, score each candidate channel 1 to 5 on four criteria and weight them by stage.

| Criterion | Question | Launch weight | Traction weight | Scale weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Audience reach | Are our buyers there in this market? | 0.35 | 0.25 | 0.20 |
| Speed to signal | How fast will we know if it works? | 0.30 | 0.20 | 0.10 |
| Expected efficiency | What CAC do we expect versus the band? | 0.15 | 0.30 | 0.35 |
| Scalability | Can it absorb two to five times the spend? | 0.20 | 0.25 | 0.35 |

`channel score = sum(criterion score x stage weight)`

Rank channels by score. The top two become primary. Anything that scores well but has never been tried becomes a test with a fixed budget and a stop date.

## Rules

1. **Two primary channels per market, maximum.** More than that and no channel gets enough budget to learn.
2. **Every test has a budget cap, a success threshold and a stop date** written before launch.
3. **Re-score quarterly** or when a market changes stage.
4. **A channel can be primary in one market and absent in another.** That is the point of the matrix.
