# Example: Toolbase (fictional)

Toolbase is a **fictional** B2B marketplace that connects independent hardware stores with tool and building-materials wholesalers. It started in Argentina and is now running three markets at three different stages. Every number here is invented to show how the framework fits together.

| Market | Stage | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Argentina | scale | Home market, known brand, CAC payback 7 months, retention proven |
| Chile | traction | 14 months live, two channels inside the CAC band, first cohorts look healthy |
| Peru | launch | Live for 10 weeks, gate 1 passed, CAC still unknown |

## Files

- `markets.yaml`: the config for this company, derived from `config/markets.example.yaml`
- `budget-q3.md`: how the quarterly budget was split using the stage model
- `country-playbook-peru.md`: the launch market's playbook
- `weekly-review-week-32.md`: one weekly review, including a cross-market rollout decision

## The loop in one paragraph

The central team fixed CAC, payback and conversion definitions before Peru launched, so Peru's numbers were comparable from week one. Budget for the quarter was split by stage weight (5 / 3 / 1). In week 32, Chile's test of a "first order delivered in 48 hours" message beat the control on lead-to-qualified rate; the weekly review logged it as a rollout candidate for Peru, where delivery speed came up in 9 of 14 customer interviews. Argentina's paid social showed rising marginal CAC, so 15% of its prospecting budget was flagged to move into CRM win-back for the monthly reallocation.
