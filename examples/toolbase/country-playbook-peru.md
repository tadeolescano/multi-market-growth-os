# Country playbook: Peru (fictional)

> Owner: market lead PE · Reviewed by: head of growth · Stage: launch · Entered launch: week 22

## 1. Market snapshot

| Item | Value | Source / confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Target businesses | ~11,000 independent hardware stores, 70% small | Trade association directory, medium |
| Main competitors | Two regional distributors with field sales; one generalist marketplace | Interviews + desk research, high |
| Share of voice | Under 5% on category search terms | Search tool estimate, low |
| Gates passed | Gate 0, gate 1 | Entry checklist |

## 2. Buyers

| Segment | Size band | Top 3 pain points | Who decides | Buying cycle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Neighbourhood store | Small | Stock-outs, delivery delays, cash flow | Owner | Weekly, by phone or chat |
| Multi-branch store | Mid | Price comparison across suppliers, invoicing, credit terms | Owner + buyer | Bi-weekly |
| Regional chain | Large | Volume pricing, reliability, integration with their system | Purchasing team | Monthly, with negotiation |

## 3. Seasonality

Peaks in April to May (construction season after the rains) and August (mid-year bonuses fund home projects). December is low for B2B ordering.

## 4. Positioning adaptation

| Central pillar | Local expression | Proof point |
|---|---|---|
| Everything you sell, one order | "Your whole supplier list in one app" | Catalogue of 8,000 SKUs from local wholesalers |
| Delivered when you need it | "Order today, on your shelf in 48 hours" in Lima | Delivery SLA from the logistics partner |
| Fair prices | Lead with transparent price comparison, not discounts | Price-match screenshots from pilot stores |
